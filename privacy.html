Effective date: 22 October 2026. This policy covers the PulseCue iPhone and Apple Watch applications.

Data collected by the app developer

PulseCue does not include advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. The app does not require an account and does not send your timer settings to a developer-operated server. The developer does not collect personal data through the app.

On-device storage and transfer

Timer preferences, active session state and transfer confirmation identifiers are stored locally. Active session state includes the timer plan, elapsed time, save time and delivered-cue identifiers so an interrupted session can be restored. When you choose Send to Watch, settings are exchanged with your paired Watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity service. Session diagnostic events are held in app memory for troubleshooting and are not automatically uploaded.

Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases and may provide platform diagnostics according to your Apple settings. This is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. PulseCue does not receive payment card details.

Health and location

PulseCue does not request HealthKit access, record workouts, read heart-rate data or track your location.

Deletion

Uninstall the app to remove its local settings. Because the devices keep their own copies, remove the app from both iPhone and Watch if you want to remove both copies.

This website

These support pages have no advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and technical access logs to operate and secure the website. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Such infrastructure processing is separate from the timer app’s on-device settings.

Changes

If app behavior changes, this policy will be updated before related data practices are introduced. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For app support or privacy questions, email buko74@icloud.com. Please include your Watch model, watchOS version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.
